# Known limitations and deliberate exclusions

- Exchange-wide completeness is not claimed. Primary-document discovery depends on publicly retrievable company/exchange PDFs and a link directory. Restricted or failed sources remain visible in the sync ledger. A successful document import is not a complete corporate-announcement feed.
- Phone notifications and browser push subscriptions are not implemented. The earlier private host task retained a single next run instead of its requested recurrence; continuous scheduling is unverified. This redesign did not change hosting or schedules. Protected batch jobs and durable in-app notifications function locally; production replacement and GitHub pushes await user review.
- Historical replay is the original March–April 2025 market window. Later imported historical filings use their actual dates and custom windows. Current prices are retrieved completed EOD sessions, not intraday quotes.
- Interactive PDFs default to 8 MB/500 pages with bounded configuration. The administrative original-file importer allows up to 32 MB/1,000 pages in resumable batches. Scanned documents need a separate OCR workflow. Large seed annual reports explicitly use partial selected pages. Ordinary permitted redirects are supported; access controls are never bypassed.
- Gemini requires a real server-side key and a supported configured model. Quotas can interrupt processing. Provider-generated facts/claims can be rejected even when JSON is valid. Retrieval/extractive fallback is labelled honestly.
- Automated source checks are conservative heuristics, not complete semantic entailment or an audit opinion. AI-extracted table facts remain source-checked rather than human-reviewed. Ambiguous periods or definitions require review.
- The deterministic extractors support clear INR-crore prose, explicitly delimited period-labelled tables, and a limited grammar of quantified guidance. Nine/six-month periods are not silently treated as full years. Arbitrary financial table layouts are not guaranteed; absent PBT/EBITDA are not fabricated.
- Guidance matching is intentionally exact on metric/unit/basis/period. This can miss synonymous definitions rather than risk comparing incompatible figures. No automated currency conversion, unit conversion or approximate-target pass/fail inference is performed.
- Full multi-year coverage, semantic embeddings/reranking, automated biographies, universal table/OCR extraction, and exchange-wide polling remain outside this small prototype. Research indicates evidence gaps for unsupported sections.
- Anonymous device cookies isolate sessions without login; they are not a multi-device authenticated account or authorization for confidential investor data. The application stores only public company evidence and local visitor preferences.
- SQLite/D1 remains the supported runtime. A different host would need a durable compatible adapter; ephemeral serverless filesystem storage is unsuitable.

- Registered automatic sources cover NTPC, the current SBI quarter, Maruti CMS records, and ITC individually dated original-disclosure cards. SBI quarter registration expires at December 2026; Maruti has many undated records and an original PDF returned HTTP 406. Historical discovery resumes in bounded pages, and dated pending records remain queued. Other companies have best-effort original-link discovery and a protected-import fallback, not a complete exchange feed. Directory links without authoritative publication dates remain candidates rather than invented events.
- Research discovery considers at most 80 permitted directory candidates and attempts one new document per interactive refresh. Ranked remaining candidates are stored as durable jobs, processed at most two per protected request. Important inaccessible/oversized candidates remain recorded. Larger source coverage requires further controlled refreshes or administrative original-file imports.
- New events use source-derived identity. Separately scoped same-category events on one page require distinct exact event quotes; an editorial headline alone cannot reliably distinguish them. Earlier IDs and provenance remain available during reconciliation.
- Relative materiality uses a unique event-role amount and a dated, source-checked, compatible annual company measure when available. Otherwise the explanation retains absolute or unknown magnitude. This does not predict stock-price impact.
- Jobs process at most three companies, two new filings/company and 30 owners per request. Operators must consume continuation cursors to finish larger batches. No continuous cloud scheduler has been enabled.
- First uncached company research can take a minute or longer while bounded public-document processing and model calls complete. Cached responses are faster; independent dashboard APIs continue to work. Failed/partial coverage remains explicit.
- Parsing numeric table columns and source labels remains deliberately conservative. Operating profit is not automatically EBITDA; unverified or conflicting records do not enter calculations. Complex/scanned layouts and synonymous-but-incompatible guidance can remain unresolved.

- The completed-session calendar is verified for NSE in 2026, including the January 15 amendment and February 1 budget session. Current sessions require provider metadata, complete valid observations and a 20-minute post-close buffer. Calendar updates are required for a future year; future holiday/provider behavior cannot be guaranteed from local tests.
- Automatic same-category splitting selects one supporting passage per category; distinct same-category actions may still require separate reviewed imports. Generic unsupported events remain review items, never invented material events.
- Some issuer URLs are mutable and reused. URL-level candidate uniqueness and content hashes do not provide an immutable exchange-wide change log; changed documents at reused URLs may require an explicit verified import/review. Maruti CMS timestamps are company-recorded timestamps, not exchange broadcast times.
- The background PDF worker allows 32 MB and the retained configurable 500-page default (maximum 1,000); administrator local-file import remains available for files requiring separate processing. A 324-page Electrosteel report indexed 323 nonempty text pages and is correctly marked partial. No reliable annual financial metrics were accepted from that layout; text availability does not imply successful financial extraction.
- Fresh investor demo preparation is reproducible from bundled original source pages. Live data and Gemini still depend on permitted external sources, credentials, model availability and quotas.

- The latest local redesign verification is in [INSTITUTIONAL_REDESIGN_REPORT.md](INSTITUTIONAL_REDESIGN_REPORT.md). Its final direct Gemini retry passed research/guidance but failed risk analysis; cold Polycab financial extraction timed out and remains failed/retryable. Production sign-off remains pending these live-provider acceptance gaps.
- Durable research is advanced in bounded Worker phases by browser polling/resubmission. Closing the page pauses progress between phases; this is not an independent autonomous research queue. Call invitations, recording notices and newspaper-publication notices are distinguished from the actual financial results/transcript; unavailable document types stay explicit.
