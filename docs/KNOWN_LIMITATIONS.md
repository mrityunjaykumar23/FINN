# Known limitations and deliberate exclusions

- Exchange-wide completeness is not claimed. Primary-document discovery depends on publicly retrievable company/exchange PDFs and a link directory. Restricted or failed sources remain visible in the sync ledger. A successful document import is not a complete corporate-announcement feed.
- No cloud schedule, phone notification or browser push subscription has been activated. Protected batch jobs and durable in-app notifications function locally. Deployment and GitHub pushes await user review.
- Historical replay is the original March–April 2025 market window. Later imported historical filings use their actual dates and custom windows. Current prices are retrieved completed EOD sessions, not intraday quotes.
- Interactive PDFs default to 8 MB/500 pages with bounded configuration. The administrative original-file importer allows up to 32 MB/1,000 pages in resumable batches. Scanned documents need a separate OCR workflow. Large seed annual reports explicitly use partial selected pages. Ordinary permitted redirects are supported; access controls are never bypassed.
- Gemini requires a real server-side key and a supported configured model. Quotas can interrupt processing. Provider-generated facts/claims can be rejected even when JSON is valid. Retrieval/extractive fallback is labelled honestly.
- Automated source checks are conservative heuristics, not complete semantic entailment or an audit opinion. AI-extracted table facts remain source-checked rather than human-reviewed. Ambiguous periods or definitions require review.
- The deterministic extractors support clear INR-crore prose, explicitly delimited period-labelled tables, and a limited grammar of quantified guidance. Nine/six-month periods are not silently treated as full years. Arbitrary financial table layouts are not guaranteed; absent PBT/EBITDA are not fabricated.
- Guidance matching is intentionally exact on metric/unit/basis/period. This can miss synonymous definitions rather than risk comparing incompatible figures. No automated currency conversion, unit conversion or approximate-target pass/fail inference is performed.
- Full multi-year coverage, semantic embeddings/reranking, automated biographies, universal table/OCR extraction, and exchange-wide polling remain outside this small prototype. Research indicates evidence gaps for unsupported sections.
- Anonymous device cookies isolate sessions without login; they are not a multi-device authenticated account or authorization for confidential investor data. The application stores only public company evidence and local visitor preferences.
- SQLite/D1 remains the supported runtime. A different host would need a durable compatible adapter; ephemeral serverless filesystem storage is unsuitable.

- The registered official-table announcement adapter currently covers NTPC. Its initial run samples two latest accessible filings; old history is not automatically backfilled. Other companies have best-effort original-link discovery and a verified protected-import fallback, not a complete exchange feed. Directory links without authoritative publication dates remain candidates rather than invented events.
- Interactive discovery considers at most 80 permitted candidates and indexes four new documents per refresh. Important inaccessible/oversized candidates remain recorded. Larger source coverage requires further controlled refreshes or administrative original-file imports.
- New events use source-derived identity. Separately scoped same-category events on one page require distinct exact event quotes; an editorial headline alone cannot reliably distinguish them. Earlier IDs and provenance remain available during reconciliation.
- Monetary materiality bands use absolute disclosed amounts. They do not establish size relative to an issuer without compatible financial context. Unknown magnitude receives a neutral score.
- Jobs process at most three companies, two new filings/company and 30 owners per request. Operators must consume continuation cursors to finish larger batches. No continuous cloud scheduler has been enabled.
- First uncached company research can take a minute or longer while bounded public-document processing and model calls complete. Cached responses are faster; independent dashboard APIs continue to work. Failed/partial coverage remains explicit.
- Parsing numeric table columns and source labels remains deliberately conservative. Operating profit is not automatically EBITDA; unverified or conflicting records do not enter calculations. Complex/scanned layouts and synonymous-but-incompatible guidance can remain unresolved.
