# Three-minute demo

1. Open the historical replay. The default calendar-day briefing is 22 April 2025. It surfaces HCLTech results, groups its supporting earnings-call context below the noise line, and calculates SBIN's relative-volume trigger from authentic daily observations.
2. Select **7 days**. Infosys ranks above HCLTech. Open **Inspect evidence**. Check the IFRS quarterly table, source-page links, supporting January call, and transparent score breakdown. Missing EBITDA remains **Not reported**.
3. Ask **Did management deliver on its FY2025 revenue guidance?** Research computes the 0.3 percentage-point miss against the revised lower bound and links both the call and subsequent result.
4. Open **Promise tracker**. Compare revenue guidance and operating-margin delivery. Expand both source passages. The score does not infer deception or share-price direction.
5. Open **Research** and select **ASIANPAINT**, **PERSISTENT** or **DMART**. Ask about revenue, cash flow or risks. These companies do not need to belong to the portfolio. Without a model key, the response consists of source excerpts and supported deterministic calculations.
6. Use **Find a company** and enter a different valid NSE ticker. FINN attempts validation, primary-link discovery and PDF indexing. A live test of `COFORGE.NS` retrieved and cited an authentic BSE document. Access failures and size limits remain visible.
7. Add Asian Paints to the portfolio, reload, and remove it. The change persists in the backend.
8. Change the price threshold to 1% in **Alert rules**, save, and revisit the briefing. More real historical observations now meet the rule. Restore your preferred value when finished.
9. Try a future empty date range or an unsupported/malformed ticker. The app explains the evidence gap instead of filling the page with fabricated developments.

Live mode is separate. Use the company refresh control to fetch completed recent sessions. The current Indian calendar day is deliberately excluded because the public chart can include an unfinished trading session. Live exchange announcements require protected ingestion; there is no claim of a complete continuously polled exchange feed.
